# Candidate ingredients considered

Four single-ingredient ideas were weighed for this picture:

- **Mozzarella** — the classic cheese pull. When a grilled sandwich is torn in two, the melted cheese stretches into long shiny threads that everyone recognises instantly.
- **Egg yolk** — a runny yolk breaking over toast. Rich colour, but the moment reads as breakfast rather than one pure flavour.
- **Honey** — a slow drizzle catching the light. Pretty, yet thin streams can vanish at small sizes and read as generic sweetness.
- **Butter** — a pat melting into warm bread. Comforting, but visually quiet; the melt is subtle and easy to miss.

## Why mozzarella won

The cheese pull is the only candidate that stays legible in a tiny thumbnail: bright golden strands against dark toast form a bold diagonal gesture. The rising steam sells heat, the toasted crust adds crunch by contrast, and the whole frame is about one thing only — stretched mozzarella at the edge of snapping. That instant readability is what makes a viewer taste it before thinking.
