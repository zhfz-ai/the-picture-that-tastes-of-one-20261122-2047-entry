# Candidates — one ingredient

Four single-ingredient ideas were weighed before committing:

- **Mozzarella (chosen)** — the cheese pull is instantly readable: two bread halves, long glossy strands, drips and steam. Texture sells itself.
- **Egg yolk** — a broken yolk is pretty but static; in flat vector it reads as a yellow circle and needs a plate story.
- **Honey** — drip from a dipper is elegant, but thin amber lines vanish at small size.
- **Butter** — a melting pat is simple and clean, yet low contrast on toast and easy to mistake for cheese without the pull.

Mozzarella won because the stretch itself is the picture: motion, gloss and appetite in one gesture, legible even as a thumbnail.
