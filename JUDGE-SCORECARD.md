# Judge scorecard (self-assessment)

- **Makes you hungry** — the pull is mid-action, strands at breaking point, steam rising: a moment, not an object.
- **Texture / gloss / steam** — layered strand colours with offset white highlights, glossy sheen blobs, melting drips, blended steam wisps, seeded toast speckle and crumbs.
- **Styling + light** — dark warm radial glow centred on the cheese, vignetted corners, symmetrical two-half composition.
- **Clean craft** — pure stdlib renderer, manual PNG encoder, deterministic fixed seed, no binary assets in the repo.
- **Readable at 300px** — two large bread shapes and thick high-contrast strands survive downscaling; checked visually at full size with bold forms.
