One ingredient: mozzarella. The program writes a single 1024x1024 picture to /output/entry.png — a grilled cheese sandwich pulled apart, long glossy strands of melted mozzarella stretched between golden toasted sourdough, steam rising. Six candidate pictures were generated and scored against the brief's five criteria in order (see CANDIDATES.md and JUDGE-SCORECARD.md); the cheese pull won on criterion 1 (you can taste the mozzarella at one look), criterion 2 (melt, gloss, steam), criterion 4 (clean craft, no artefacts) and criterion 5 (reads instantly at 300 pixels).
